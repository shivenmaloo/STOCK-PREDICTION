Backtesting

Simulated trading on the model's out-of-sample predictions, compared against simply buying and holding.

Retrains models across ~15 historical time windows to simulate real out-of-sample trading — takes anywhere from under a minute to a couple minutes. Every weighting and sizing decision only ever uses information that was actually available at that point in time, the same rule the whole app is built on.

Enter a ticker above and run a backtest.

TCN Research

Experimental: a causal Temporal Convolutional Network plus a configurable risk-management pipeline — a research and paper-trading feature, not a promise of profitable live trading.

PyTorch isn't installed, so this module is unavailable — everything else in the app works normally without it. Install torch to enable it.

Trains a fresh TCN across ~15 walk-forward windows per config, same discipline as the main Backtesting page — can take a few minutes. "Search for a better config" trains 2 new candidate architectures and keeps whichever genuinely validates best out-of-sample.

Volatility Surface

Real Black-Scholes implied volatility, solved from live options chains — not a trained model, a mathematical inversion of actual market prices.

Fetches live options chains, filters out illiquid/arbitrage-violating quotes, solves for implied volatility per contract, and interpolates a smooth surface. Not every ticker has listed options.

Predictions

Ensemble ML forecasts with walk-forward validated confidence — never a guarantee, always shown with uncertainty.

First prediction for a ticker trains models from scratch (walk-forward validated across ~5 years of history) and can take anywhere from under a minute to several minutes depending on your machine — the LSTM model (if PyTorch is installed) is the slowest part. After that, results are cached for 24 hours. The risk profile only changes the suggested position size — never the prediction itself.

Enter a ticker above and click Predict.
